**Legal baseline (EU/UK).** The Database Directive 96/9/EC gives a *sui generis* right against substantial extraction from databases built with substantial investment. *Innoweb* (C-202/12) made dedicated meta-search over a protected database infringing. *Ryanair v PR Aviation* (C-30/14) lets sites enforce anti-scraping T&Cs where no database right applies [5]. Practical rule: take only offer facts (product, price, dates), never images or full catalogues, and link back to the source. Switzerland has no database right; in BGE 131 III 384, scraping with real own effort was held not unfair under UWG 5c [8].

**Competition note.** In every country there are flyer aggregators (kaufDA/Bonial, marktguru, Folderz, PromoPromo, Tiendeo, Blix) and community deal sites (the Pepper network: mydealz, Dealabs, HotUKDeals, Pepper.pl). A local, student-first scope is the differentiator. It is weakest in the UK (UNiDAYS/Student Beans).

How the scores were set:
- **Reuse** = German/French/Italian already handled in CH, plus shared chains (Aldi/Hofer, Lidl, MediaMarkt, Zalando, Amazon.de, Galaxus.de).
- **Monetisation** is driven mainly by affiliate spend: DE €932M and UK £1.8bn [10] versus low CPM/CPC in PL and PT [7].

## 3. Recommended order and first 3 cities

1. **Germany** (best score, almost free reuse of CH-DE; 2–3 weeks): **Munich** (closest, high purchasing power, ~130k students), **Berlin** (~200k), **Cologne** (~100k). Next: Hamburg, Frankfurt, Konstanz (cross-border with St. Gallen).
2. **Austria** (cheapest add-on: same language and same affiliates; ~1–2 weeks): **Vienna** (~190k students), **Graz**, **Innsbruck** (near St. Gallen/Vorarlberg).
3. **France** (big market, CH-FR reuse; budget for anti-bot and CNIL consent): **Paris**, **Lyon** (near Geneva), **Toulouse**. Next: Lille, Bordeaux.
4. **Netherlands** (best structured data, English-friendly students): **Amsterdam**, **Utrecht**, **Rotterdam**. Next: Groningen, Leiden.
   - *Bonus:* **Belgium** right after, almost free once FR and NL connectors exist (Leuven, Ghent, Brussels).
5. **Italy** (large student base, strong volantini, Ticino reuse; lower CPM): **Milan**, **Rome**, **Bologna**. Next: Turin, Padua.

**Not now:**
- **UK:** high revenue but crowded student-discount market, weak flyer culture, separate law.
- **Nordics:** 4 countries' worth of effort.
- **Poland:** strong promo culture but low ad and affiliate value. Re-evaluate once premium alerts prove out.
